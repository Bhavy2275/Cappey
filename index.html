ABOUT CAPPEY INDIA

From planning to handover

Cappey India Private Limited supplies and installs elevator and audio-visual systems for projects across India. We work with building owners, architects and contractors from planning through handover.

Our work ranges from selecting a lift and coordinating its installation to planning room audio, displays and controls. We can also help with ongoing service after handover.
