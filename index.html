MARKETS WE SERVE

Sound and sight for every kind of space

From the first meeting to a full auditorium, we shape each system around its people, architecture and everyday use.

Conference & Boardrooms

PTZ auto-tracking cameras, beamforming tabletop and ceiling array mics, touch screen automation, and acoustic wall panels.

Auditoriums & Halls

Turbosound line array systems, digital audio processors, 4K high-lumen laser projectors, and stage lighting automation.

Schools & Institutions

Interactive flat panel displays, smart digital lecterns, distributed classroom speakers, and centralized campus announcements.

Clubs & Nightclubs

High-SPL line array speakers, bone-rattling subwoofers, Pioneer DJ pro consoles, and synchronized LED video walls.

Houses of Worship

Acoustic clarity for complex reverberant halls, steerable column line arrays, feedback suppressors, and broadcast cameras.

Recording Studios

Genelec active studio monitors, analog mixing consoles, modular audio interfaces, and studio-grade XLR wiring.

Restaurants & Dining

Immersive multi-zone background audio, weather-resistant outdoor garden speakers, and digital signage menu matrices.

Gyms & Fitness Centers

Moisture-resistant high-output ceiling speakers, cardio theater wireless audio transmitters, and dynamic workout lighting.
